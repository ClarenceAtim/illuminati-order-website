Membership Benefits

WHAT MEMBERS DEDICATE THEMSELVES TO

The Illuminati is a powerful and carefully guarded organization. Its members commit themselves to three pursuits, held always in service of the human species.

◆

WEALTH

Be ever prepared. Live this moment as though it is your last moment on this earth and acquire the maximum spiritual wealth here and now. Guided by your soul, you work toward an abundance of valuable assets — held as a responsibility to create goodness for humanity, never as an end in itself.

◆

POWER

It is through faith that we move from the known to the unknown. Members seek influence — economic, technological, and cultural — so that sorrow, pain, grief, and fear may be eliminated, and all may know happiness and peace instead of suffering.

◆

FAME

Those who devote themselves fully to the advancement of humanity earn a lasting name. Successful people stay on their toes at all times — and a life lived for the species is remembered with honour in your community, your country, and the world at large.
